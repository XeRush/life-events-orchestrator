For families in Dubai the residence visa is filed with GDRFA-Dubai (usually through an Amer centre); elsewhere with
ICP. It takes 3-10 days and cannot start until the child's passport exists. It needs the child's passport, the
attested birth certificate, the sponsoring parent's residence visa and Emirates ID details, and a photograph.

Every submission LifeLoop prepares is released by an Amer officer from the officer dashboard. The visa approver at
GDRFA or ICP makes the determination; LifeLoop never predicts or states an approval before the authority confirms it.
No visa fee is in LifeLoop's sources.
