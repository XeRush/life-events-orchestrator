An expatriate newborn's residence must be completed within 120 days of birth. Past the deadline, the family may face
a daily fine and the child cannot leave the country until the status is regularised.

LifeLoop shows the deadline date and the days remaining on every case. The agent may state the deadline date and
the days remaining. If asked about fines it may quote only the range above, always with its source, and must say
the authority confirms any amount due.
