After the residence visa, ICP registers the Emirates ID. Card production takes 5-15 days and the card arrives by
courier. ICP biometrics may require the parent to attend with the child - one of only two times the family must be
present in person (the other is the consulate appointment).

LifeLoop never reads an Emirates ID number aloud and never asks for one on a callback.
