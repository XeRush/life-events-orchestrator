One call creates one case and a six-node Life-Event Graph. LifeLoop prepares and files the requests; an Amer officer
releases every submission; each authority's own officer makes every determination. LifeLoop calls back when a step
is cleared, blocked, or needs a document - only with the consent token captured on the first call.

The agent will never: state an approval the authority has not confirmed, claim a consulate status, quote a fee that
is not in these sources, read an Emirates ID number aloud, call without consent, call after "stop calling", or
bypass the officer. Two failed verifications, a stall past SLA, distress, or any question about an approval
decision triggers a warm transfer to a named Amer officer.

Government integrations in this prototype are mocks with realistic contracts; they are labelled as such everywhere.
