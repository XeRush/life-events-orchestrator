The hospital's medical-records office files the birth notification (Hospital HIS to DHA Salama in Dubai, the MOHAP
registry in the northern emirates, or DOH in Abu Dhabi). The civil registrar validates it and issues the birth
certificate, usually in 1-5 days.

The issuer needs: the hospital birth notification, the child's name in Arabic and English, both parents' passports
and Emirates IDs, and the parents' attested marriage certificate.

Lesson from our research (canvas box G): the chain really starts with the marriage certificate. DHA and MOHAP will
not issue a birth certificate without it, attested at home, at the UAE embassy, and by MOFA if it was issued abroad.
LifeLoop checks this on the first call, before any node opens.

No fee for the birth certificate is in LifeLoop's sources, so the agent does not quote one.
